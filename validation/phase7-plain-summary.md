# VaultTec — Phase 7 overnight test: the plain version

Tested overnight on past prices, paper money only — no real money was used, nothing was bought or sold for real.

## What I tested

Five things, all on old price charts the system had never been picked on before:

1. **The combined system on two brand-new time windows** — spring and summer 2022, when prices were mostly falling. The combined system is the main system plus the trend-follower running together in one pot.
2. **Crypto on its own** — just Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana, on their own, including weekends (crypto never sleeps).
3. **Six extra big company stocks** the system had never been tested on — AMD, Netflix, Broadcom, Costco, Home Depot and Visa — to check the system works on more than just the usual 12.
4. **The trend-follower on its own in the 2022 falling market** — the one gap we had never checked.
5. **The same tests with small pots of money** — $20, $50 and $100 instead of $10,000 — to see what a small pot really does in dollars and cents.

## The answers, in plain words

**1. Combined system on the new windows: NOT TESTED yet.** The two new time windows need price data that could not be pulled overnight, so there is no result here — not a good result and not a bad one. It is still waiting to be tested.

**2. Crypto on its own:** the RSI version held up, the Bollinger version held up, and the trend-follower held up. Pooled across the windows that ran: RSI made up $648.93 (19 trades), Bollinger made up $606.89 (12 trades), trend-follower made up $792.96 (46 trades). Solana's price data did not come through cleanly on every window, so its numbers are only partial — see the full report. Only 4 of the 6 windows ran, so this is a partial answer — the rest still need their price data pulled.

**3. The six extra stocks: NOT TESTED yet.** None of the six new stocks had price data saved, and it could not be pulled overnight, so there is no result here yet.

**4. Trend-follower in the 2022 falling market: NOT TESTED yet.** The falling window did not run overnight, so this gap is still open — no result here yet.

**5. Small pots of money:** a $20 pot was up $0.77 per window on average on the main system (65 trades across the windows that ran, and 8 trades could not be placed at all because the pot was too small for those stocks); a $50 pot was up $2.02 per window on average on the main system (70 trades across the windows that ran, and 3 trades could not be placed at all because the pot was too small for those stocks); a $100 pot was up $4.04 per window on average on the main system (73 trades across the windows that ran). For comparison, the $10,000 pot was up $408.75 per window on average. In plain words: a $20 pot moves by cents, not dollars — even a good six months does not turn $20 into real money, and some stocks cannot be traded at all with a pot that small.

## What changed

- The combined system on the new windows was NOT tested overnight — the price data could not be pulled, so nothing changed there yet.
- The trend-follower in the falling market was NOT tested overnight either — that gap is still open.
- The six extra stocks were NOT tested overnight — the price data could not be pulled.
- We now know what small pots really do in dollars and cents — a $20 pot moves by cents, and some stocks cannot be traded at all with a pot that small.

## What stays the same

- The main system is still wide-6 mean-reversion (RSI + Bollinger). Nothing in this test replaces it.
- The combined system is NOT adopted. It still needs live paper trades over real days before anyone trusts it with anything.
- No real money, no live trades, no broker. Everything here is paper money on old charts.
- Past results do not promise future results. A good night on old charts is not a promise about tomorrow.

## What happens next

- The parts that were not tested get re-run once the price data comes through — that is the new windows, the extra stocks, and the small pots on those windows.
- Keep watching the live paper trades — that is the real test, one day at a time.
- If the combined system keeps looking good on live paper too, that is when it earns a second look. Not before.
- The legal review still has to happen before any real money or any public launch.
